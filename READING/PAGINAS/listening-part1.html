B. Exam task — Track 1

You will hear people talking in eight different situations. Choose the best answer (A, B or C).
Question 1

You hear a woman telling her son about her favourite schoolteacher.

What did the woman like about her history teacher?

A   his acting skills
B   his sense of humour
C   his passion for the subject

Your answer:

C ✓ The woman says her teacher was 'so into history' and everything he said stuck with her — that's passion for the subject, not acting or jokes.
Question 2

You hear a girl talking about maths.

What does the girl enjoy most about maths?

A   getting clear answers
B   applying it to everyday life
C   solving difficult questions

Your answer:

A ✓ The girl says maths is 'either right or wrong' and you can 'always get a hundred per cent' — she likes that there is a clear, certain answer.
Question 3

You hear a man telling a friend about being unable to study art at school.

He says that he would have liked to study art in order to

A   know more about art history.
B   learn some techniques.
C   follow a career in art.

Your answer:

B ✓ The man says it would have been useful to know 'how to apply different paints properly' — he means learning practical skills, not art history or a career.
Question 4

You hear two students discussing a sports class they have just attended.

What do they agree on about the class?

A   how relevant the information was for them
B   how knowledgeable the teacher was
C   how inspiring the new ideas were

Your answer:

B ✓ Both students mention their teacher 'had done tons of research' — they agree on how much he knew, not on the ideas or how useful the class was.
Question 5

You hear a girl telling a friend about her brother.

How does the girl's brother feel about starting university?

A   anxious about meeting new people
B   worried about managing the workload
C   concerned about his abilities in his subject

Your answer:

A ✓ The girl says her brother isn't 'as confident as he'd have expected' about 'getting to know the others' — that's nerves about meeting new people.
Question 6

You hear a student talking to his teacher about an assignment.

The boy says that he feels

A   uneasy about having to write about an unfamiliar topic.
B   unsure about how to carry out some research.
C   doubtful about which writing style to use.

Your answer:

C ✓ The boy says he doesn't know 'what formal language should be' for the blog — he's unsure about the writing style, not the topic or the research.
Question 7

You hear a science teacher talking to her class.

What is she doing?

A   cancelling an activity / a plan
B   changing an arrangement
C   correcting some information

Your answer:

C ✓ The teacher clears up a 'misunderstanding' about where classes will be held (gym, not the main hall) — she's correcting information.
Question 8

You hear two friends discussing learning foreign languages.

They both hold the opinion that

A   communication does not rely on speaking a language.
B   it's possible to learn about culture through language.
C   learning languages is essential for travel.

Your answer:

B ✓ Both friends agree you learn about 'other countries' way of life, and their history' through language — that's culture, not travel or communication itself.

C. Choose the correct form of the adjective

Unit 2 · Hobbies and Leisure
Question 1

1. How people can walk along a tightrope I'll never know – it's .

astonishing ✓ The tightrope walking itself causes the feeling, so we use the -ing form.
Question 2

2. I've just started climbing. I've always found it to watch.

fascinating ✓ The activity (climbing) causes the feeling, so we use the -ing form.
Question 3

3. Watching the ice-skaters on the pond kept me for a couple of hours.

entertained ✓ The person watching feels this way, so we use the -ed form.
Question 4

4. I've been trying to finish this puzzle for ages – I can't do it and it's so !

irritating ✓ The puzzle causes the feeling, so we use the -ing form.
Question 5

5. Ben was when he got back from playing football. He slept for an hour.

exhausted ✓ Ben feels this way after playing football, so we use the -ed form.
Question 6

6. What a brilliant programme. I feel really to take up a new hobby.

motivated ✓ The person feels this way after watching the programme, so we use the -ed form.
Question 7

7. Have you seen that new action film? It's !

thrilling ✓ The film causes the feeling, so we use the -ing form.
Question 8

8. I found my new piano teacher very . I feel much better about my playing.

encouraging ✓ The teacher causes the feeling in the speaker, so we use the -ing form.

D. Complete the question tags

Question 1

1. You go swimming a lot, you?

don't ✓ The main sentence is positive ("You go"), so the tag is negative.
Question 2

2. You'll come with me to the theatre, you?

won't ✓ The main sentence is positive, so the tag is negative, using the same auxiliary (will).
Question 3

3. You've done some interesting things, you?

haven't ✓ The main sentence is positive, so the tag is negative, using the same auxiliary (have).
Question 4

4. You're going surfing this weekend, you?

aren't ✓ The main sentence is positive, so the tag is negative, using the same auxiliary (are).

E. Complete with question words

Question 1

1. 's your favourite band?

Who ✓ We ask about a person (a band) with who.
Question 2

2. kind of films do you like?

What ✓ We ask about a type of thing (films) with what kind of.
Question 3

3. would be your perfect weekend?

What ✓ We ask someone to describe a thing (a weekend) with what.
Question 4

4. do you spend your free time?

How ✓ We ask about the way something is done with how.

F. Exam task — Track 2

You will hear people talking in eight different situations. Choose the best answer (A, B or C).
Question 1

You hear an announcement about a band called the Big Bang.

What is the purpose of the announcement?

A   to offer music lessons
B   to promote a concert
C   to recruit band members

Your answer:

A ✓ The band is holding workshops where people 'can learn to play some of their tunes' — that's offering music lessons.
Question 2

You hear a young woman telling her friend about an article she has read.

After reading it, the woman says that she felt

A   convinced to try a new activity.
B   encouraged to continue doing an activity.
C   keen to avoid one particular activity.

Your answer:

B ✓ After reading the article, she says she's 'no longer concerned' and is 'going to buy a new game' — she feels encouraged to keep doing it.
Question 3

You hear two friends discussing free time.

They agree that it is important to

A   spend time outside the house.
B   do nothing sometimes.
C   try out a range of activities.

Your answer:

A ✓ They agree that 'getting out of the house for a walk' helps them relax — spending time outside is what matters to them.
Question 4

You hear a boy telling a friend about the sport of diving.

What does he say about it?

A   Jumping from the highest board is frightening.
B   Completing a successful dive is motivating.
C   Watching professionals dive is thrilling.

Your answer:

C ✓ The boy is excited by the idea of watching experienced divers perform — he finds it thrilling to watch.
Question 5

You hear two friends talking about hiking.

What is the man's primary reason for trying it?

A   to see more of where he lives
B   to be physically fitter
C   to overcome a fear

Your answer:

A ✓ His main reason for hiking is enjoying and discovering more of the area where he lives, rather than fitness or fear.
Question 6

You hear a man telling a friend about producing furniture as a hobby.

Why does the man enjoy working with wood?

A   He takes pleasure in producing something useful.
B   He likes recycling existing pieces of furniture.
C   He appreciates the opportunity to be creative.

Your answer:

C ✓ He enjoys working with wood mainly because it lets him be creative and design his own pieces.
Question 7

You hear a review of a film.

What is the reviewer's opinion of it?

A   the ending was disappointing
B   the scenery was breathtaking
C   the acting was unconvincing

Your answer:

B ✓ The reviewer praises how the film looked rather than the plot or acting — the scenery was breathtaking.
Question 8

You hear two friends talking about taking photos to put on social media websites.

What does the boy say he likes about it?

A   trying different techniques
B   receiving positive comments
C   analysing his friends' photos

Your answer:

A ✓ He likes experimenting with different photo techniques rather than the comments he receives or looking at others' photos.

H. Exam task — Track 3

You will hear people talking in eight different situations. Choose the best answer (A, B or C).
Question 1

You hear a teacher talking to her class.

What is she doing?

A   explaining the results of a competition
B   thanking them for taking part in an event
C   encouraging them to complete a challenge

Your answer:

C ✓ The teacher says 'keep moving' and 'you don't have to go to the sports centre' — she's encouraging everyone to keep up the daily steps challenge.
Question 2

You hear two friends talking about learning to ski.

What do they agree about?

A   how much fun the classes are
B   how difficult it is to learn the techniques
C   how physically tiring the activities can be

Your answer:

B ✓ One says she 'can't seem to turn the turns right' and the other agrees 'Nor can I' — they agree the technique is hard to learn.
Question 3

You hear a sports instructor talking to an athletics class.

What does he say about the current long jump record?

A   It hasn't been broken for a long time.
B   It will be difficult to break.
C   It's something he has tried to break himself.

Your answer:

A ✓ He says 'it's quite a while since the current record was set' — it hasn't been broken for a long time.
Question 4

You hear a girl telling a friend about lessons on eating and exercising that she has done at school.

How does she feel about what she has learned?

A   doubtful that some of the advice will benefit her
B   surprised by some of the information
C   keen to try out a suggestion

Your answer:

B ✓ She says the fact you can eat more carbs if you exercise 'was new to me' — she was surprised by this information.
Question 5

You hear a student talking to his sports teacher about getting fit.

What would the boy like to do?

A   take part in a competitive sport
B   use some gym equipment
C   find a training partner

Your answer:

A ✓ He already uses the gym and has a training partner — what he'd now like is to enter competitive events.
Question 6

You hear two friends talking about a TV programme they have watched.

What does the woman think about it?

A   It contained some useful tips.
B   It raised surprising arguments.
C   It discussed interesting new research.

Your answer:

A ✓ She says it 'was good to be reminded' of the sleep tips they already knew — the programme gave useful tips.
Question 7

You hear an expert talking about what being healthy really means.

She believes that many people have a mistaken idea about

A   how important social contact is.
B   how much exercise they need.
C   how important it is to have a good diet.

Your answer:

C ✓ She says people know about exercise and social contact but not about proper nutrition — the mistaken idea is about diet.
Question 8

You hear a fitness expert talking about warming up before exercise.

He says that people don't always warm up because they

A   have not been educated about its importance.
B   want their exercise sessions to be quick.
C   don't enjoy preparation exercises.

Your answer:

B ✓ She says people struggle to 'fit it in between work or study and dinner time' — they skip warming up because they want to save time.

I. Get it right!

Agreeing with a negative statement
Question 1

“I don't like maths.” “ do I.”

Neither ✓ We use neither + do/does/did (or nor + do/does/did) to agree with a negative statement. 'So do I' is only for agreeing with a positive statement.
