Practice Test 2 — Extra Practice

A short review of the grammar and vocabulary from Practice Test 2.

A. Fixed phrases

Based on Part 1 — Multiple-choice Cloze

Word bank: against · fact · few · pressure · save · up

Question 1

Only a lucky of students got a place on the trip.

few ✓ A lucky few means a small group of lucky people.
Question 2

In , the second half of the film was much better than the first.

fact ✓ In fact is the normal way to add emphasis.
Question 3

Many parents used to argue letting kids use phones at school.

against ✓ We say argue against something.
Question 4

If we leave early, we can time on the journey.

save ✓ Save time means you use your time well.
Question 5

She refused to give even when things got difficult.

up ✓ Give up means to stop trying.
Question 6

The team is under to finish the project by Friday.

pressure ✓ Under pressure to do something is the normal phrase.

B. Small grammar words

Based on Part 2 — Open Cloze
Question 1

At some during the meeting, everyone stopped listening.

point ✓ At some point means “at a moment we don’t know”.
Question 2

I never been to Japan, but I'd love to go.

have ✓ Have + past participle shows the action is part of your life experience up to now.
Question 3

She has new laptop for work.

a ✓ We use a before a word that starts with a consonant sound, like “laptop”.
Question 4

Can you attach the file your email?

to ✓ Attach something to something is the normal phrase.
Question 5

The committee decided to postpone the meeting.

has ✓ “Committee” is treated as one thing here, so it goes with has.

C. Word formation

Based on Part 3 — Word Formation
Question 1 — SWITCH

He's good at between different languages quickly.

switching ✓ After “good at” we need the -ing form. SWITCH → switching.
Question 2 — REAL

In , the plan was far more complicated than we expected.

reality ✓ In reality is the normal phrase. REAL → reality.
Question 3 — PERFORM

Interruptions can cause a real drop in .

performance ✓ We need a noun after “a drop in”. PERFORM → performance.
Question 4 — SURPRISE

, the exam was easier than everyone had predicted.

Surprisingly ✓ This word describes the whole sentence. SURPRISE → surprisingly.
Question 5 — USE

Some habits are completely and should be dropped.

useless ✓ A negative word after “completely”. USE → useless.
Question 6 — PRACTICE

The teacher gave us some tips for the exam.

practical ✓ A describing word before “tips”. PRACTICE → practical.

D. Key word transformations

Based on Part 4 — Key Word Transformations
Question 1

It isn't necessary for you to bring anything.

NEED

You anything.

don't need to bring ✓ We use don’t need to + verb to say something is not necessary.
Question 2

She began teaching here three years ago.

FOR

She here for three years.

has taught ✓ We use has + past participle + for to say how long something has continued up to now.
Question 3

'Don't run in the corridor!' the teacher told us.

WARNED

The teacher in the corridor.

warned us not to run ✓ We use warn someone not to + verb to report an order telling someone not to do something.
Question 4

I regret not saving more money last year.

WISH

I more money last year.

wish I had saved ✓ We use wish + had + past participle to talk about regret about the past.
Question 5

The airline cancelled the flight because of the fog.

CALLED

The flight because of the fog.

was called off ✓ We use was/were + called off to say something was cancelled.
Question 6

Despite feeling tired, she finished the race.

ALTHOUGH

, she finished the race.

Although she felt tired ✓ We use although + subject + verb to contrast two ideas. “Although” needs a full sentence after it, not just a noun like “despite” does.
