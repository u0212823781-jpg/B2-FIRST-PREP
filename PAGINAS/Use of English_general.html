04

Exam-smart tips

Part 1

Think in chunks

  • Read the whole sentence first.
  • Look for collocations and fixed expressions.
  • Check the words before and after the gap.
  • Eliminate options that don't fit the context.
  • Don't choose a word just because you know it.
Part 2

Grammar matters

  • Think grammar, not vocabulary.
  • Read before and after the gap.
  • Look for grammar clues: prepositions, articles, pronouns and linkers.
  • Remember: only ONE word per gap.
  • Read the completed sentence again.
Part 3

Build the word

  • Identify the part of speech you need.
  • Decide whether the meaning should be positive or negative.
  • Look for clues before and after the gap.
  • Watch out for spelling changes.
  • Always change the word given — never leave it unchanged.
Part 4

Protect the meaning

  • Keep the same meaning.
  • Use the key word and do not change it.
  • Use 2–5 words, including the key word.
  • Identify the grammar structure being tested.
  • Check tense, person and singular/plural.
  • Never change the part of the sentence already given.
