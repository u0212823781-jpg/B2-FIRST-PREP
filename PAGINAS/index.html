Everything you need to know about your B2 First exam.

01

Exam structure

The exam assesses 5 skills across 4 papers and takes approximately 3 hours and 30 minutes.

Use of English

  • 4 parts
  • 30 questions
  • ~20 min
TARGET · 18/28

Reading

  • 3 parts
  • 22 questions
  • ~55 min
TARGET · 24/42

Writing

  • 2 parts
  • 2 tasks
  • 1h 20 min
TARGET · 24/40

Listening

  • 4 parts
  • 30 questions
  • 40 min
TARGET · 18/30

Speaking

  • 4 parts
  • 4 tasks
  • 14 min per pair
TARGET · 36/60

Practice guide: these figures are approximate B2 targets for practice tests. Your official result is based on your overall Cambridge English Scale score, with 160 as the B2 threshold.

03

Exam Day

  • Arrive on time
  • Bring ID, pens, pencil, eraser & sharpener
  • No electronic devices or watches
  • Listen carefully to instructions
  • Manage your time carefully
04

Results & Certificates

Keep your Confirmation of Entry — you'll need it to check your results online.

Follow the instructions provided to access your results.

Results are usually available in 4–6 weeks.
