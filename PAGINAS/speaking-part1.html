Speaking · Part 1

Interview

The examiner will ask you questions about yourself, your life, your studies and your interests.
Answer naturally and give enough detail.

1. Useful Language

Express Yourself Naturally
  • As far as I'm concerned, ...
  • To be honest, ...
  • The way I see it, ...
  • I really enjoy / I'm quite into ...
  • What I like most about ... is ...
  • A big part of my day is ...
  • I'd say I'm more of a ... person.
See all useful phrases →

2. Go Beyond a Short Answer

Basic

I live in Madrid.

Better

I really like where I live because it's a lively city.

Opinion → Reason → Detail
B2

I live in Madrid, and what I like most about it is the mix of culture, green spaces and the very active lifestyle.

3. Make It Personal

Add a Personal Touch
  • Give real reasons.
  • Use examples from your life.
  • Mention your experiences and feelings.
  • Avoid memorised answers.
  • Be specific, not general.
★Small details make your answers more natural and interesting.

4. Dealing With Unexpected Questions

Buy Yourself Time
  • That's an interesting question.
  • I've never really thought about that, but ...
  • Well, off the top of my head, I'd say ...
  • I suppose it depends on ...
  • One way of looking at it is ...
★Pause, think, then answer. It's better to speak clearly than to rush.

What do you usually do in your free time?

I usually spend my free time doing a mix of things. I enjoy staying active, so I often go for a run or to the gym. For example, at the weekend I sometimes go hiking with friends.
At the same time, I also like relaxing at home, reading or watching a good series.
