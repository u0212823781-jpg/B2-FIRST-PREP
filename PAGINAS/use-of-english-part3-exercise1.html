Use of English · Part 3 · Word Formation

Exercise 1

Online shopping has become increasingly (1) ___ (POPULARITY) over the last decade, changing the way people buy everyday items. One of its main (2) ___ (ADVANTAGE) is convenience: customers can compare prices without leaving their homes. However, this (3) ___ (GROW) trend also raises some concerns. Many small shops have found it increasingly (4) ___ (DIFFICULTY) to compete with large online retailers, and some have had to close (5) ___ (COMPLETE). There is also the question of (6) ___ (SECURE) — sharing personal and banking details online always carries some risk. Despite these (7) ___ (DISADVANTAGE), most people agree that online shopping is now an (8) ___ (REPLACE) part of modern life.

Question 1 — POPULARITY
popular ✓ POPULARITY (noun) → popular (adjective). We need an adjective after become.
Question 2 — ADVANTAGE
advantages ✓ ADVANTAGE → advantages (plural noun) — "one of its main..." needs a plural noun.
Question 3 — GROW
growing ✓ GROW → growing (adjective / present participle), describing the noun "trend".
Question 4 — DIFFICULTY
difficult ✓ DIFFICULTY (noun) → difficult (adjective), needed after "found it".
Question 5 — COMPLETE
completely ✓ COMPLETE (adjective) → completely (adverb), modifying the verb "close".
Question 6 — SECURE
security ✓ SECURE (adjective) → security (noun), after "the question of".
Question 7 — DISADVANTAGE
disadvantages ✓ DISADVANTAGE → disadvantages (plural noun), after "these".
Question 8 — REPLACE
irreplaceable ✓ REPLACE (verb) → irreplaceable (adjective), with the negative prefix ir- and suffix -able.
