Use of English · Part 1

Multiple-choice Cloze

Understand the technique, spot the pattern, and practise until it becomes automatic.

How does it work?

You read a text with 8 gaps.

For each gap, choose the correct answer from four options (A, B, C or D).

Part 1 mainly tests how well you understand vocabulary in context and how words naturally combine.

What should you look for?

Collocations & dependent prepositions

✓ make a decision
interested in · depend on

Words that naturally go together, including the fixed preposition that follows certain adjectives and verbs.

Phrasal verbs

find out → discover
turn down → refuse

Verbs + particles with a specific meaning.

Fixed expressions & idioms

in spite of · on purpose
once in a blue moon

Established combinations and idiomatic phrases native speakers use as a single unit.

Meaning & vocabulary in context

journey / trip / travel
say / tell / speak / talk

Choose the word with the exact meaning and register required by the context.

Spot the clue

The company ____ a decision to change its policy.

Adid
Bmade
Ctook
Dput
What is the clue?

the word decision

Why?

We say make a decision. It is a collocation.
