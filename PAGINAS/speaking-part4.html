Speaking · Part 4

Discussion

You and your partner will discuss more abstract ideas and topics in depth.
Speak together for 4–5 minutes.

1. Useful language

Expressing a View

  • From my perspective, ...
  • As far as I'm concerned, ...
  • The way I see it, ...
  • I'd argue that ...
  • I tend to feel that ...
  • It seems to me that ...
  • I'd say it largely depends on ...

Giving Reasons & Examples

  • The main reason for this is ...
  • This is largely because ...
  • One example that comes to mind is ...
  • A good example of this would be ...
  • This is particularly true when ...
  • Take ..., for instance.
  • What I mean by that is ...

Qualifying Your Opinion

  • To some extent, ...
  • In most cases, ...
  • Generally speaking, ...
  • That depends largely on ...
  • There are exceptions, of course, but ...
  • I wouldn't necessarily say that ...
  • It may be true that ..., but ...
  • Although that's often the case, ...

Speculating & Considering Possibilities

  • It could be argued that ...
  • One possible explanation is ...
  • This might be due to ...
  • There's a good chance that ...
  • It's quite likely that ...
  • I suppose it would depend on ...

2. Go Beyond Your First Answer

Basic

Yes, people use technology too much.

Better

People probably spend too much time using technology because they use their phones for almost everything.

B2

To some extent, I'd say people rely too heavily on technology nowadays, particularly smartphones. This is largely because so many everyday activities, from communicating to entertainment, now take place online.

Opinion → Reason → Explanation / Example

3. When the Answer Isn't Yes or No

It really depends on the situation. For younger people, social media may be an important way of keeping in touch, whereas for others it can become an unnecessary distraction.
To some extent, yes. However, I wouldn't say this is true for everyone.
★Strong answers are often nuanced, not simply yes or no.

4. Build on the Discussion

Refer to Your Partner
  • As you mentioned earlier, ...
  • I'd agree with what you said about ...
  • Building on that idea, ...
  • That's an interesting point because ...
  • I see what you mean, although ...
  • I'd look at that slightly differently.
Add Another Perspective
  • Another way of looking at it is ...
  • On the other hand, ...
  • Having said that, ...
  • At the same time, ...
  • We should also bear in mind that ...
  • There's another side to this as well.

5. Dealing With Challenging Questions

Buy a Little Thinking Time
  • That's an interesting question.
  • I've never really thought about that, but ...
  • That's quite difficult to answer.
  • Off the top of my head, I'd say ...
  • I suppose one way of looking at it is ...
Then develop
Think→Position→Reason→Example

See It in Action

To a large extent, yes. Technology has made many everyday tasks much more convenient, particularly when it comes to communication and accessing information. For instance, people can now work, study or even manage their finances without leaving home. Having said that, this convenience can also make us overly dependent on technology and create problems when we spend too much time online.
