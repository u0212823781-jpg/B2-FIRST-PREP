Speaking · Part 2

Long Turn

You will compare two photographs and answer the question above them.
Speak for 1 minute.

1. Useful language

Comparing & Contrasting

  • Both pictures show...
  • In both pictures...
  • One similarity is that...
  • While in the first picture..., in the second...
  • Whereas...
  • Unlike the first picture...

Speculating

  • They might be...
  • They could be...
  • Perhaps...
  • It looks as if...
  • They seem to be...
  • I imagine they might...
  • It's possible that...

Developing Ideas

  • This could be because...
  • One possible reason is that...
  • This might mean that...
  • They may feel... because...
  • This would probably make them feel...
  • Another possibility is that...

Answering the Question

  • I'd say...
  • I think the main reason might be...
  • In my opinion, ... would probably...
  • I imagine that...
  • Overall, I'd say...

2. Describe or compare?

✕Just describing

In the first picture, there are some people in an office. They are sitting at a table and looking at a computer. In the second picture, there is a man working outside.

VS
✓Comparing

Both pictures show people at work, but the situations are quite different. While the people in the first picture are working together indoors, the man in the second picture seems to be working alone outside.

★Connect the two photos instead of talking about them separately.

3. Build a stronger answer

Basic

The people are working together.

Better

The people seem to be working together on a project.

B2

The people seem to be working together on a project, which might make the task easier because they can share ideas and help each other.

★Add speculation and reasons to develop your ideas and show a wider range of language.

7. Your partner's turn

Listen to your partner's long turn. Then answer the examiner's question about their photographs.

Examiner

Which way of working do you think is more difficult? Why?

You

I'd say working outside is probably more difficult because you have to deal with things like bad weather. For example, if it's extremely hot or raining heavily, it could make the job much harder and more tiring.

About 30 secondsGive a clear answer, explain why and add a little detail.
