C. Exam task — Track 10

Interview with travel writer Anna Bryant. Choose the best answer (A, B or C).
Question 1

Before travelling to another country, Anna always tries to

A   watch people practising their traditions.
B   talk to someone from that country.
C   do some background reading.

Your answer:

B ✓ She explains what first drew her to the subject in a general way — her answer is about her personal interest, not one single dramatic event.
Question 2

How does Anna feel about her language skills?

A   regretful that she didn't pay more attention at school
B   confident that she can communicate fairly easily
C   amazed by how many languages she has acquired

Your answer:

B ✓ She talks about the mix of different traditions she found when she started, and how that variety is what shaped her approach to the work.
Question 3

Anna says that when visiting someone in their home

A   it's fine to let them know you're anxious.
B   it's a good idea to copy how they behave.
C   it's advisable to find out what to do in advance.

Your answer:

C ✓ She describes the biggest challenge as a practical, everyday one in her work, rather than a problem with funding or with other people.
Question 4

How did Anna feel when she made a mistake?

A   annoyed that she had forgotten some advice
B   grateful that her host was sympathetic
C   amused by her own behaviour

Your answer:

B ✓ She says the reaction from local people was warmer and more welcoming than she expected — that's what surprised her most.
Question 5

How did Anna overcome culture shock when she lived abroad?

A   by studying the culture carefully
B   by getting to know local people
C   by establishing a routine

Your answer:

C ✓ She says the most rewarding part is seeing a tradition passed on to a new generation, not the recognition she personally receives.
Question 6

How did Anna feel when she was at the Lantern Festival?

A   astonished that she had never heard about it
B   anxious to remember every moment of it
C   eager to participate in it

Your answer:

C ✓ Her advice to someone starting out is to be patient and to take time to build trust with people, rather than to focus on qualifications first.
Question 7

What does Anna say about the book she is writing about culture?

A   She is disappointed in her progress so far.
B   She is keen to get feedback from people she knows.
C   She is unsure about including her own experiences.

Your answer:

A ✓ Looking ahead, she says her main hope is that the traditions she studies will still be alive and practised in the future.

E. Exam task — Track 11

Interview with Liam Banks about a clean-up day. Choose the best answer (A, B or C).
Question 1

When did Liam decide to get involved in cleaning up his town?

A   when neighbours asked him to join in
B   when he studied the environment at school
C   when the local council asked for volunteers

Your answer:

B ✓ He says he got into the subject after seeing the direct effect of pollution somewhere close to home.
Question 2

How did Liam feel while he was looking for volunteers?

A   surprised by the amount of support he got
B   concerned about how to inform people
C   pleased to be offered free materials

Your answer:

B ✓ He says the hardest part of his job is convincing people to change habits — a practical, everyday obstacle.
Question 3

On the morning of the clean-up, Liam felt

A   admiration for the people of his town.
B   respect for the other organisers.
C   delighted at the number of reporters there.

Your answer:

A ✓ He explains that a small, local project actually made the biggest difference, more than a big, wide public campaign.
Question 4

How did Liam feel when he was cleaning the pond?

A   upset at the thought of animals being harmed
B   angry at the people who had left rubbish there
C   worried about how to make sure it stayed clean

Your answer:

B ✓ He says what keeps him motivated is seeing real change happen in a community.
Question 5

What disappointed Liam and the other organisers on the day?

A   volunteers complaining about the work
B   not having time to meet all the volunteers
C   forgetting to arrange food for volunteers

Your answer:

C ✓ His advice for someone wanting to help is to start with small, everyday changes, rather than waiting to do something huge.
Question 6

How does Liam feel about his involvement in the clean-up?

A   proud that he has made a positive difference
B   keen to organise further environmental projects
C   confident he can improve things on a wider scale

Your answer:

Question 7

B   Read about issues affecting the Earth.
C   Look out for local environmental events.

Your answer:

A ✓ Looking to the future, he feels a cautious kind of hope, based on seeing more young people getting involved.

Your answer:

B ✓ He says he started the sport almost by accident, going along with a friend, rather than following family tradition or a coach spotting him.
Question 2

How did Max feel before his first competition?

A   confident that he would enjoy taking part
B   surprised by how many spectators there were
C   nervous about competing against experienced divers

Your answer:

A ✓ He says the hardest adjustment when he moved up a level was mainly mental, rather than the physical training.
Question 3

What does Max say about doing arm stand dives?

A   He found them challenging at first.
B   He now prefers them to other dives.
C   He only performs them when he has to.

A ✓ He picks out one particular match as his proudest moment, more than any award ceremony or personal best.
Question 4

What was Max worried about when he had to change a dive?

A   not remembering what he had to do
B   not having had enough time to practise it
C   not being physically able to do it

Max enjoys his practice sessions because he

A   gets to see what everyone else is doing.

A   take part in a major competition
B   learn to dive with a partner
C   beat his best score
