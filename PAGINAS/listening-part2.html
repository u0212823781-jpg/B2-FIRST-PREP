One long monologue, ten gaps to complete. Vocabulary and grammar from Units 1–3, plus the three exam tasks, all self-checking.

Word bank

Unit 1 · Food and Drink
balanced · catering · chop · consume · portion · swallow · taste · vitamin

Before the exam task, think about which of these words are nouns, which are verbs, and which are adjectives. Some words fit more than one column (e.g. taste can be a noun or a verb, and catering can be a noun or an adjective).

D. Linking words — complete the gaps

Question 1

1. Olivia did well to get the job her lack of experience.

Question 2

2. I can drive to work that I've bought my first car.

now ✓ "Now that" introduces a new situation (buying the car).
Question 3

3. Ben should do well at work that he works hard.

provided ✓ "Provided that" introduces a condition (working hard).
Question 4

4. I'd like to have a creative job I'm not sure what exactly!

though ✓ Introduces a contrast (not being sure exactly what job).
Question 5

5. My sister's got a part-time job I work full time.

whereas ✓ Introduces a contrast between the two sisters’ jobs.
Question 6

6. I'll be able to save for a holiday I've got a job.

once ✓ Means "as soon as" he has a job.
Question 7

7. Matt's reached the stage in his career he'd like to be promoted.

where ✓ Introduces a description of the stage in his career.
Question 8

Question 1
