03

The four Speaking parts

Part 1

Interview

  • Personal questions
  • Everyday topics
  • Giving information
  • Expressing opinions
Part 2

Long turn

  • 2 photographs
  • Compare & contrast
  • Answer the question
  • Speak for 1 minute
Part 3

Collaborative task

  • Discuss the options
  • Give & justify opinions
  • Agree & disagree
  • Make a decision together
Part 4

Discussion

  • Part 3 topic
  • Develop your answers
  • Give reasons & examples
  • Justify your opinions
04

Smart exam tips

Part 1
  • Develop your answers — avoid yes/no
  • Give a reason or example
  • Use a range of vocabulary
  • Relax — talk naturally about yourself
Part 2
  • Compare, don't just describe
  • Answer the question above the photos
  • Use speculative language
  • Speak for the full minute
Part 3
  • Interact with your partner
  • Give your opinion and ask for theirs
  • Agree & disagree politely
  • Reach a decision together
Part 4
  • Give developed answers
  • Explain why you think something
  • Give reasons & examples
  • Listen & respond to your partner
