C. Choose the correct alternative (past tenses)

Question 1

1. I wish more fashionable clothes when I was younger.

I'd worn ✓ Wish + had + past participle is for regretting something in the past.
Question 2

2. My brother into designer clothes but he isn't bothered about them now.

used to be ✓ Used to describes a past state (being into something); would is only for repeated actions, not states.
Question 3

3. you just come back from shopping? Where did you go?

Have ✓ Present perfect (Have you just...) is for something that happened very recently.
Question 4

4. I in the queue when a woman came up and pushed in front of me!

was standing ✓ Past continuous for the action in progress when something else happened.
Question 5

5. When I got to the shops, Mum for me.

had already been waiting ✓ 'Already' with a duration before another past event needs the past perfect — her waiting started and finished before you arrived.
Question 6

6. the internet for bargains all morning.

I've been browsing ✓ 'All morning' needs the present perfect continuous, for something that started earlier and continues up to now.
Question 7

7. I've missed the deadline for getting those cool boots in the online sale – it at 12 o'clock.

was ✓ 'Was at 12 o'clock' refers to a specific, finished point in time, so we use the simple past.
Question 8

8. online all morning looking at clothes! Why don't we go out now?

You've been ✓ Present perfect continuous for an activity going on over the recent period (all morning).

F. Complete with used to / be used to / get used to

Question 1

1. My grandparents live abroad and we would visit them every summer.

used to ✓ A past habit/state that is no longer true.
Question 2

2. Did you play 'hide and seek' at home when you were a child?

use to ✓ After "Did", we use the base form use to, without -d.
Question 3

3. I'm living in a city, so I'd never move to the countryside.

used to ✓ Be used to + -ing means being familiar with something.
Question 4

4. I to like our old house 'cos it was too far from where my friends lived.

didn't use ✓ The negative of used to also drops the -d after didn't.
Question 5

5. My sister moved out of my parents' house and is living on her own.

getting used to ✓ Get used to + -ing means becoming familiar with something (a process).
Question 6

6. They the size of the house now, though it seemed enormous when they moved in.

are used to ✓ Be/get used to for a situation you are now familiar with.
Question 7

7. you use to live next door to me when we were kids?

Did ✓ Question form of used to needs did + use to.
Question 8

8. I never sharing a bedroom, so I'm glad I've got my own again now we've moved.

got used to ✓ Get used to for becoming familiar with something over time.
