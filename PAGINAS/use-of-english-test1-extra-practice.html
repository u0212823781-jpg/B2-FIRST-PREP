Practice Test 1 — Extra Practice

A short review of the grammar and vocabulary from Practice Test 1.

A. Fixed phrases

Based on Part 1 — Multiple-choice Cloze

Word bank: at · in · off · out · suggested · up

Question 1

After weeks of thinking about it, he finally made his mind to change jobs.

up ✓ We use the fixed phrase make up your mind to say someone decides.
Question 2

The plane took two hours late because of the storm.

off ✓ A plane takes off when it leaves the ground.
Question 3

Her tone of voice that she wasn't happy with the decision.

suggested ✓ A tone of voice can suggest a feeling without saying it directly.
Question 4

We finally arrived the campsite just before it got dark.

at ✓ We arrive at a specific place.
Question 5

It turned that the museum was closed on Mondays.

out ✓ Turn out to be means “in the end, it was”.
Question 6

He's been love with the idea of moving abroad for years.

in ✓ We fall in love with something or someone.

B. Small grammar words

Based on Part 2 — Open Cloze
Question 1

I didn't find what had really happened until much later.

out ✓ We say find out to mean “discover something”.
Question 2

They’ve lived that same street for over twenty years.

on ✓ We say live on a street.
Question 3

Nobody was quite sure had caused the fire.

what ✓ After sure we need a question word to introduce what happened.
Question 4

I need to speak the teacher before the lesson starts.

to ✓ We say speak to someone.
Question 5

Could you look my essay before I hand it in?

at ✓ We say look at something to mean you direct your attention to it.

C. Word formation

Based on Part 3 — Word Formation
Question 1 — VOLUNTEER

She's involved in several projects at weekends.

voluntary ✓ A describing word for “projects”. VOLUNTEER → voluntary.
Question 2 — ACHIEVE

Winning the scholarship was a huge for him.

achievement ✓ A noun after “a huge”. ACHIEVE → achievement.
Question 3 — SURPRISE

, the test was easier than we expected.

Surprisingly ✓ A word that describes the whole sentence. SURPRISE → surprisingly.
Question 4 — MAJOR

The of students passed the exam.

majority ✓ A noun after “the”, making “the majority”. MAJOR → majority.
Question 5 — COMFORT

Moving to a new country can be a stressful but experience.

uncomfortable ✓ A describing word with a negative meaning. COMFORT → uncomfortable.

D. Key word transformations

Based on Part 4 — Key Word Transformations
Question 1

I have never eaten sushi before.

FIRST

This is eaten sushi.

the first time i've ✓ We use This is the first time I've + past participle to say something is happening for the first time.
Question 2

Someone broke the window yesterday.

BROKEN

The window yesterday.

was broken ✓ We use was/were + past participle to say something happened to a thing, when we don’t say who did it.
Question 3

'I'll call you later,' she said.

WOULD

She said that she me later.

would call ✓ In reported speech, “will” changes to “would”.
Question 4

It's possible that he forgot the meeting.

MAY

He the meeting.

may have forgotten ✓ We use may + have + past participle to guess about something that happened in the past.
Question 5

I last spoke to her two months ago.

SPOKEN

I for two months.

haven't spoken to her ✓ We use haven’t + past participle + for to say how long something hasn’t happened.
Question 6

This book is more interesting than the last one.

AS

The last book this one.

wasn't as interesting as ✓ We use not as + adjective + as to compare two things and say one is less than the other.
