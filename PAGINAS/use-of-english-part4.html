Use of English · Part 4

Key Word Transformations

Same meaning, different words — rebuild the sentence around the key word you're given.

How does it work?

You are given an original sentence, a second sentence with a gap, and a key word.

You must complete the second sentence so that it has the same meaning as the first — using the key word.

6 questions The key word must not be changed Use 2–5 words, including the key word Up to 2 marks per question

What should you look for?

Grammar changes

active → passive
direct → reported

Recognise when the sentence needs a different grammatical structure.

Fixed expressions & collocations

make up your mind
in charge of

Look for expressions that can communicate the same meaning in a different way.

Phrasal verbs

cancel → call off
reduce → cut down on

Be ready to replace a verb or expression with an appropriate phrasal verb.

Word patterns

verb + -ing / to
adjective + preposition

Pay attention to structures such as verb + infinitive, verb + -ing, prepositions and adjective patterns.

Whatever pattern you use, the completed sentence must always communicate the same meaning as the original.

Spot the clue

Passive voice

Someone stole my bike last night.
BY

was stolen by someone
Reported speech

"I'll call you tomorrow," she said.
WOULD

would call
Comparatives

This exam is more difficult than the last one.
AS

wasn't as difficult as
Modal verbs

It isn't necessary for you to finish today.
HAVE

don't have to
Conditionals

If you don't leave now, you'll miss the train.
UNLESS

unless you leave
